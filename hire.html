Terms of Service

Freelance HVAC design services by Mohd Kaif Khan ("Designer") for the client named in the quote ("Client"). Version 1.1.

1. Scope of work

Work is limited to the scope selected in your quote (Concept Design, Detailed Design, or Design + Site Support) for HVAC only. Electrical, plumbing, fire fighting, structural and civil design are not included. Work outside the agreed scope or area is quoted separately.

2. Fees & payment

The quote is an estimate based on building type, area and scope. The final fee is confirmed in writing after the Designer reviews your drawings and requirements. Payment is made in milestones: 50% advance before work begins; 20% after delivery of the load summary and equipment selection; 20% after delivery of drawings; and 10% after final approval. Late payments may pause work. Taxes (including GST, where applicable) are charged extra as required by law.

3. Timeline

The timeline starts only after the advance is received and the Client has supplied the required inputs (architectural drawings, requirements, site details). Delays in providing inputs or approvals extend the timeline accordingly.

4. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate, complete and timely information, and give feedback within a reasonable time. The Designer is not responsible for errors caused by incorrect or changed inputs.

5. Revisions

The revisions included in the fee depend on the selected scope: Concept Design — 2 revisions; Drawings Only — 2 revisions; Detailed Design — 5 revisions; Design + Site Support — 5 revisions. Further revisions, or changes caused by a new layout or a change of requirements after approval, are charged separately.

6. Deliverables & ownership

Deliverables are issued in PDF at each milestone, and the matching payment falls due on delivery. The final approved set, and any editable files (e.g. CAD, only if agreed in writing), are released once all milestone payments are received. On full payment, the Client may use the deliverables for the project they were prepared for. The Designer keeps the right to reuse general know-how and may show non-confidential work in a portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.

7. Site support

The "Design + Site Support" scope includes 2 site visits within the NCR region. Additional visits, and travel outside NCR, are charged separately. Site visits are not included in the other scopes.

8. Cancellation & refunds

If the Client cancels before work starts, the advance is refunded less payment-gateway charges. Once work has started, the advance and any milestone payments already made are non-refundable, and the Client pays for work completed up to the cancellation date.

9. Confidentiality

Both parties will keep the other's non-public project and business information confidential and use it only for this project.

10. Liability

The design is prepared with reasonable professional care using the information provided. Final responsibility for installation, equipment procurement, site execution and approvals from statutory authorities rests with the Client and their contractors. To the extent permitted by law, the Designer's total liability for any claim is limited to the fees paid for the work in question, and the Designer is not liable for indirect or consequential losses.

11. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of India. The parties will first try to settle any dispute in good faith; unresolved disputes fall under the jurisdiction of the courts at New Delhi.
