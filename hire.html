Terms of Service

Version 2.2 · Last updated 7 October 2026. These terms are between Mohd Kaif Khan, an independent freelance HVAC designer ("Designer"), and the person or business named in the quote ("Client").

1. Agreement

Ticking "I agree", paying any amount, or sending a quote request by WhatsApp/email means the Client accepts these terms and the quote (building type, built-up area, scope, fee and payment stages). Electronic acceptance is intended to be binding. Work begins only after the advance is received.

2. Scope of work

Services are HVAC design only, limited to the scope in the quote:
• Concept Design — heat load summary and equipment selection.
• Drawings Design — HVAC layout drawing.
• Detailed Design — single-line HVAC scheme drawing, heat load calculations, and equipment selection with drawings.
• Design + Site Visit — everything in Detailed Design plus 2 site visits.
Not included unless agreed in writing: electrical, plumbing, fire fighting, structural or civil design, other trades, statutory approvals or NOCs, procurement, supervision, testing and commissioning. Extra work is quoted separately.

3. Fees & payment

The fee is estimated from the building type and built-up area the Client provides. If the actual area, usage or building type differs, or the drawings show a different scope, the fee is adjusted and confirmed in writing. Payment stages: Concept Design and Drawings Design — 50% advance, 50% on submission of the final deliverable. Detailed Design and Design + Site Visit — 20% advance; 30% on submission of the single-line HVAC scheme drawing; 40% on submission of heat load calculations/equipment with drawings; 10% after final approval of drawings and calculations. Each stage payment is due within 7 days of submission. The next stage may be paused until payment is received. A minimum project fee of ₹2,000 applies to residential and commercial projects. GST and other taxes are extra if applicable. Payment is by UPI or the payment link shared; any bank/gateway charges are borne by the payer. Payment queries must be raised in writing within 7 days.

4. Deliverables, approval & release

Each stage deliverable may first be shared in review form. The final issued version of a stage, and any editable files (e.g. CAD, only if agreed in writing), are released once that stage's payment, and for editable files all payments, is received. If the Client gives no written feedback within 10 days of submission (and a reminder), the stage is treated as accepted and its payment falls due.

5. Revisions

Included revisions: Concept Design — 2; Drawings Design — 2; Detailed Design — 5; Design + Site Visit — 5. A revision is one consolidated set of comments on a submitted deliverable within the original design basis. Changes to the design basis (new layout or architectural changes, load or usage changes, change of equipment type or make, new areas) are new work and quoted separately. Additional revisions beyond those included may be charged separately, with the charge confirmed before that work starts. Unused revisions lapse when the project closes.

6. Client responsibilities

The Client will provide accurate and complete inputs (architectural drawings, requirements, site details), a single point of contact, and timely feedback. The Client is responsible for verifying dimensions and site conditions, coordinating with other trades, and obtaining statutory approvals. The Designer is not responsible for errors, delays or extra work caused by incorrect, late or changed inputs. If the project is inactive for over 30 days because of the Client, the Designer may close the file; completed stages remain payable and restarting may be re-quoted.

7. Timelines

Timelines are good-faith estimates that start after the advance and required inputs are received. They extend for Client delays, scope changes and events beyond the Designer's control.

8. Site visits

Design + Site Visit includes 2 site visits within the NCR region for design coordination and verification. For other scopes, a site visit can be requested and is quoted separately; requesting a quote creates no obligation on either side. Additional visits and travel outside NCR are charged separately. Visits are not construction supervision or quality inspection of the contractor's work.

9. Intellectual property

The Designer retains copyright in all deliverables. On full payment, the Client receives a non-exclusive licence to use them for the project they were prepared for. They may not be reused for other projects or sites, or resold, without the Designer's written consent. The Client keeps ownership of materials it supplies and confirms it has the right to share them. Until full payment, deliverables are for review only and not to be used for construction or procurement. The Designer may show non-confidential project work in a portfolio unless the Client objects in writing.

10. Professional responsibility & liability

The Designer will perform the work with reasonable skill and care using the information provided. The Designer will correct errors in a deliverable at no extra charge if notified in writing within 30 days of delivery. The Designer is not responsible for contractor workmanship, installation, procurement, commissioning, site conditions not disclosed, or the Client's own use of drawings not issued final. To the extent permitted by law, total liability for any claim is limited to the fees paid for the stage or scope concerned, and neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss (such as loss of profit or delay costs). Nothing here limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or anything that cannot be limited by law.

11. Cancellation & termination

If the Client cancels before work starts, the advance is refunded less bank/gateway charges. After work starts, the advance and stages already invoiced are non-refundable, and work in progress on the current stage is charged proportionately. The Designer may pause or end the engagement if a payment is more than 7 days overdue after written notice, or for abusive conduct. If the Designer cannot complete the work, fees for stages not performed are refunded.

12. Confidentiality

Each party will keep the other's non-public project and business information confidential and use it only for this project, except where disclosure is required by law or to professional advisers bound to confidentiality.

13. Independent relationship

The Designer works as an independent contractor, not as an employee, partner or agent of the Client, may work for others, and controls how the work is done. No exclusivity applies unless agreed in writing.

14. Force majeure

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond reasonable control, including illness, natural events, power or internet outage, or government orders. Timelines extend accordingly.

15. Governing law & disputes

Indian law governs. The parties will first try to resolve a dispute in good faith for 30 days after written notice. Unresolved disputes fall under the courts at New Delhi.
