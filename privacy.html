Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 7 October 2026. This explains how Mohd Kaif Khan ("I") handle personal information collected through this quote page and during projects.

1. What I collect

Details you enter or send me: name, email, phone/WhatsApp, project name and location; your quote choices (building type, built-up area, scope); project material you share (drawings, requirements, site details); our messages; and payment confirmations you send (such as a UPI reference or screenshot). I do not collect or store your card or bank login details. Online payments are processed by your UPI app and bank, or by a third-party payment gateway, which has its own privacy policy.

2. How this page works

Information typed into the form stays in your browser and is not stored on a server by this page. It reaches me only when you choose to send it by WhatsApp or email, and those services have their own privacy policies. This page uses no advertising cookies or analytics of its own. Loading it fetches fonts from Google Fonts and a QR script from cdnjs, and the web host may log standard technical data (such as IP address), so those providers may see your visit.

3. How I use it

To prepare quotes and deliver the services, communicate with you, handle invoicing and payments, keep accounting and tax records, comply with law, and protect against or resolve disputes. I may mention non-confidential project work in my portfolio unless you object in writing.

4. Sharing

I do not sell your information. I share it only as needed with payment apps and banks, messaging, email and file-storage providers, accountants or advisers, other consultants or vendors on your project (with your agreement), and authorities where the law requires.

5. Keeping it

I keep information while the project runs and afterwards as long as needed for legal, tax, accounting and dispute purposes, then delete or anonymise it.

6. Security

I use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please share only the information needed for the project.

7. Your information about others

If you share information about other people or confidential third-party material (for example architect drawings or occupant details), you confirm you are entitled to share it with me for the project.

8. Your choices

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by emailing mohdkaifkhan.w@gmail.com. I will respond within a reasonable time. Some records must be kept by law, and withdrawing consent may mean I cannot continue the work. The same address is for any privacy complaint.

9. Children

My services are for adults and businesses, not for anyone under 18.

10. Reviews

If you send a review, I publish it only with your permission, using the name format you choose (and your role/company if you give it). Your email and phone are never published. You can ask me to edit or remove your review at any time by emailing mohdkaifkhan.w@gmail.com.

11. Law & changes

I handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to the extent it applies. I may update this policy; the version on this page applies from the date shown.
